# UC-012 — Pay and process simulated payment outcomes

## Metadata

- Status: Draft specification; new grouping, paid-reservation, expiry, identity, recovery, and reconciliation policies are **Proposed**, pending OQ-007/OQ-008/OQ-015. No payment policy is newly Accepted by this document.
- Owner: Project owner for product choices; analyst/assistant for specification.
- Scope: SC-006/SC-026; connected checkout, inventory, order, and audit dependencies SC-005/SC-007/SC-012/SC-022 in [Functional Scope](../../01-product/FUNCTIONAL_SCOPE.md).
- Source: [catalog](../USE_CASE_CATALOG.md), [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md), [UC-011](UC-011-checkout.md), [UC-007](UC-007-manage-inventory.md), and the WI-002 proposal in [Open Decisions](../OPEN_DECISIONS.md). Scope retains a simulated Payment Provider and excludes real-money movement.
- Priority: P0 — Proposed in the [catalog](../USE_CASE_CATALOG.md); confirmation remains OQ-014.

## Goal

Let the Buyer pay for an accepted checkout through a simulated Payment Provider, and give the Buyer an observable payment outcome without duplicate requests or reservation effects. Preserve verified provider evidence when the purchase can no longer safely proceed, and retain paid stock holds for later Seller confirmation.

## Primary actor

Buyer acting through the User account that owns the accepted purchase group. Exact account verification and permitted demo controls remain OQ-005.

## Supporting actors

- Simulated Payment Provider creates or recovers the same logical Payment Request and sends authenticated success, failure, or expiry evidence.
- The system checks callback authenticity/correlation, coordinates payment and reservation outcomes, and exposes an attributable status for recovery or reconciliation.
- Seller / Shop Operator later confirms each paid Shop order for fulfillment in UC-013. That Seller confirmation consumes the Shop order's paid holds and deducts its stock once; payment success does not confirm the Shop order or deduct stock.
- Authorized Internal Staff may inspect a reconciliation case within OQ-005 permissions. Refund/reversal decisions and execution remain UC-017/OQ-009/OQ-010, rather than an implicit callback side effect.

## Trigger

The Buyer initiates payment for an accepted checkout, the provider delivers a payment-result callback, a recorded payment/reservation deadline is reached, or interrupted processing is recovered.

## Preconditions

- [UC-011](UC-011-checkout.md) originally accepted the selected checkout as one purchase group, with separate Shop orders, an authoritative snapshot, exact Shop/SKU quantities, and associated holds. New payment application checks the current unpaid holds/deadline separately; completed-outcome recovery does not require them to remain unpaid. This grouping is Proposed under OQ-007, rather than acceptance of the entire cart.
- The group has a recorded payable amount/currency and expiry boundary. Exact valid amounts, currency precision, zero-payable behavior, and configured reservation duration remain OQ-002/OQ-007/OQ-008/OQ-011/OQ-012.
- Payment initiation/status access is authorized for the owning Buyer. Operating one of the Shops does not by itself grant access to another Buyer's group or payment details.
- A stable logical Payment Request identity belongs to this purchase group. A repeated initiation or recovery uses that identity; it cannot create a second payable request for the same group.
- A provider result must be authentic, structurally valid, and correlated to that Payment Request before it can change payment, order, or inventory outcomes. Claimed payment success in a Buyer-controlled return page is not verified provider evidence.

## Main flow

All new business policy in this flow is Proposed and references the canonical rules below.

1. The Buyer requests payment for the accepted purchase group. The system checks ownership and resolves the original request identity/outcome first. An already-completed request returns its established status and current Shop-order progress without new initiation or rechecking old unpaid-hold guards; conflicting instructions are refused. Only a genuinely pending/uncommitted initiation checks that the group can still wait for payment with active unpaid holds before expiry.
2. The system creates or recovers the one logical request with the simulated Payment Provider using the group's recorded amount/currency and request identity. It does not recalculate an existing group's purchase amount from current cart or catalog data.
3. The Buyer receives the same request identity, the group's payment/purchase status, and the expiry boundary. Pending means that payment has not been applied; loss of a provider or Buyer response leaves an uncertain/pending outcome rather than proving failure or success.
4. The simulated provider sends a result identifying the request and a stable logical event ID. Verify authenticity and required structure first. For an already-known authenticated event identity, compare the immutable evidence with its original record: an exact repeat returns its recorded processing result; changed facts require reconciliation linked to the original record without applying the altered payload or disclosing another purchase. A malformed/unauthenticated claim cannot create such a conflict case.
5. For a new authenticated event identity, check request/group/amount/currency correlation before any business effect. Unknown/mismatched references are rejected. A matching result for the same already-applied terminal outcome returns that established outcome/current Order progress and retains the extra evidence without reapplying effects; this applies even if the provider used a new event ID. Authentic opposite terminal evidence requires reconciliation; a genuinely new admissible pending result proceeds to its current guards. Delivery attempts remain distinct from the business event.
6. For new verified success, the system checks the authoritative group outcome and time and verifies that **every** required reservation for **every** Shop order remains `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` and unexpired. The purchase snapshot and exact reserved quantities must match the group; no sibling Shop order can be silently omitted.
7. Only if those guards still hold, record the authoritative payment-success application time and each Shop order's Seller-response deadline using the Proposed OQ-009 policy (candidate 24 hours), without extending it on replay. One complete business outcome moves the request `PENDING` → `SUCCEEDED`, all group Shop orders `PAYMENT_PENDING` → `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`, and all group reservations `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` → `ACTIVE_PAID`. The quantities stay reserved and physical stock remains unchanged. A partially paid group or partially converted hold set is not permitted. The original unpaid-payment deadline cannot release `ACTIVE_PAID` holds.
8. The Buyer receives successful payment and each Shop order's paid/awaiting-Seller-confirmation outcome. The system records provider evidence and the linked request/group/order/reservation effects for audit. Each Seller sees only the Shop order allowed by their Shop permission and may later confirm it in UC-013.

## Alternative and error flows

- **A1 — Unauthorized initiation, status, or intervention:** refuse the action without disclosing another Buyer's protected payment/purchase data or changing their request, reservations, orders, or stock. Exact staff and simulator-control permissions remain OQ-005.
- **A2 — Initiation cannot reach the provider or its response is lost:** retain an observable uncertain/pending outcome and recover by the same logical request identity and provider status. A local transport error does not prove that the provider failed to create the request. Never issue a second logical payable request merely because a response was lost. If the group expires during recovery, apply A4/A7; recovery does not extend its holds.
- **A3 — Verified payment failure:** if the group is still awaiting payment, move the request to `FAILED`, all its Shop orders to `PAYMENT_FAILED`, and release every `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` reservation once under [SM-ORDER-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-order-001). Do not deduct stock, partially mark a Shop paid, or automatically create a retry request. A conflicting result after a terminal outcome follows A8.
- **A4 — Provider expiry or authoritative unpaid-reservation expiry:** before any success business outcome has committed, expiry moves the request to `EXPIRED`, all its Shop orders to `PAYMENT_EXPIRED`, and releases its `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` holds once. No stock deduction or paid/Seller-confirmed result occurs. At or after the recorded expiry boundary, success is no longer eligible to convert unpaid holds, even if expiry cleanup or a callback worker runs late. A request already `SUCCEEDED` and its `ACTIVE_PAID` holds are not expired by this unpaid-payment timer; paid Seller timeout/cancellation policy remains OQ-008/OQ-009/OQ-010.
- **A5 — Malformed, unauthenticated, or uncorrelated callback:** reject malformed/unauthenticated evidence without payment/order/reservation/stock effects. For a new authenticated event identity, also reject unknown/mismatched request or payable amount/currency. Authenticated reuse of an already-known event identity with changed immutable facts follows A8 instead; this comparison precedes new-event correlation. Keep diagnostic evidence according to Proposed audit coverage without exposing protected Buyer data. Rejected evidence is not an admissible terminal result.
- **A6 — Exact duplicate or already-applied outcome:** return the existing event-processing result for the same provider-scoped event ID and immutable evidence. A new authenticated event ID with matching request/amount/currency and the same already-applied terminal outcome also returns the established outcome and retains evidence without another effect; do this before pending-only hold guards. No repeated paid-hold conversion, release, deduction, Order transition or effective business audit change occurs. Delivery/evidence records remain distinguishable from changes, and replay preserves later Seller progress.
- **A7 — Verified success received or recovered too late:** preserve provider-success evidence and mark reconciliation `REQUIRED` when the group is expired/failed/cancelled or unpaid holds have expired/released. Ensure remaining `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` holds release once; no group Shop order becomes paid or confirmed and no stock is deducted. A provider's claimed earlier occurrence time does not resurrect an expired group. Display the unsuccessful purchase outcome separately from the unresolved provider-payment evidence; do not report either automatic fulfillment or a completed refund. Refund/reversal handling remains OQ-009/OQ-010.
- **A8 — Conflicting evidence:** authenticated reuse of a known event ID with changed immutable evidence, or verified success versus failure/expiry for an already terminal request, requires reconciliation. Preserve the established business outcome and linked evidence; do not blindly replace a terminal result or undo/double effects. Paid/confirmed Orders and paid holds are not automatically cancelled/released/restocked by later failure/expiry evidence; an unsuccessful/cancelled purchase is not resurrected by later success. Authentic failure/expiry after an unpaid Buyer cancellation may corroborate financial non-success; preserve the local CANCELLED application/cause without another release. Authentic success after cancellation requires reconciliation; no completed refund is inferred. An original local unpaid-expiry job after success is a stale timer, not new opposite provider evidence.
- **A9 — Success versus expiry race:** resolve eligibility against the authoritative state/time when the group business outcome commits. Success may commit only while all required holds are `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` strictly before their expiry; expiry wins eligibility at the boundary. The result is either one complete paid transition with `ACTIVE_PAID` holds or one unsuccessful close/release, never both. Provider-success evidence that cannot secure the paid transition follows A7. This Proposed ordering is distinct from Accepted moderation concurrency policy OQ-003.
- **A10 — Concurrent terminal callbacks:** serialize the logical outcome through the canonical guards. Exact repeats have one business effect; opposite verified outcomes preserve the established result and require reconciliation under A8. Processing order cannot allow one Shop to become paid while another is released.
- **A11 — Interrupted outcome processing:** after recovery, inspect the same request/event identity and already recorded business outcome. Complete an eligible uncommitted outcome once, or return an existing completed outcome. If only receipt/verification was recorded before interruption and unpaid holds have since expired, use A7 rather than promising a paid transition. No externally observable partial payment/order/hold outcome may survive recovery as a valid completed payment. If success already committed, recovery preserves `ACTIVE_PAID` or later valid Seller outcomes despite the original unpaid deadline.
- **A12 — Buyer abandons or revisits payment:** abandonment is not verified payment failure and does not immediately deduct or release stock by itself. Return the same request/status on revisit while eligible; normal authoritative expiry applies. Payment failure/expiry has no automatic retry. Any deliberate new checkout must revalidate current eligibility, prices, and stock in UC-011 and creates a distinct purchase group; cancellation/retry permissions and refund effects remain OQ-009/OQ-015.
- **A13 — Product, SKU, or price changes after checkout acceptance:** use the accepted group's authoritative snapshot and holds rather than silently creating a new purchase or recalculating the amount. The effect of a later restriction/cancellation on existing groups remains OQ-004/OQ-009; accepted snapshot behavior does not grant eligibility for any new checkout.
- **A14 — Owning cancellation and paid closure:** [UC-015](UC-015-track-cancel-orders.md)/[BR-CANCEL-001](../BUSINESS_RULES.md#br-cancel-001) permit the owning Buyer to cancel a whole pending unpaid group before expiry, moving application to CANCELLED and cancelling/releasing all its Shop orders/holds together. Success-first refuses the original unpaid intent rather than converting it to paid-Shop cancellation; cancellation-first preserves local cancellation with any late-success financial exception. Creation recovery after cancellation may query the original provider identity to establish evidence, but cannot create/reinitiate a payable request. Paid per-Shop cancellation/rejection/timeout under UC-013/015 preserves group SUCCEEDED and its amount, recording a separate REQUIRED compensation obligation; no blind full-group refund or payment-state rollback.

- Proposed [BR-ORDER-001](../BUSINESS_RULES.md#br-order-001): purchase-group payment atomicity, immutable purchase facts, and paid/awaiting-confirmation separate from Seller confirmation.

- [NFR-001](../NON_FUNCTIONAL_REQUIREMENTS.md#nfr-001), [NFR-002](../NON_FUNCTIONAL_REQUIREMENTS.md#nfr-002), [NFR-003](../NON_FUNCTIONAL_REQUIREMENTS.md#nfr-003), [NFR-004](../NON_FUNCTIONAL_REQUIREMENTS.md#nfr-004), and [NFR-006](../NON_FUNCTIONAL_REQUIREMENTS.md#nfr-006) provide authorization, verified asynchronous processing, inventory consistency, audit, and observable recovery requirements. ## State/data changes

- [SM-PAYMENT-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-payment-001) owns `PENDING`/`SUCCEEDED`/`FAILED`/`EXPIRED`/`CANCELLED` outcomes and the separate reconciliation annotation `CLEAR`/`REQUIRED`/`RESOLVED`. Verified provider-payment evidence remains distinguishable from whether the purchase can proceed. Unpaid whole-group cancellation adds CANCELLED application under BR-CANCEL-001; paid Shop closure does not change SUCCEEDED and retains its distinct REQUIRED compensation obligation. A later reconciliation annotation does not replace the established payment outcome; permitted resolution authority/actions remain OQ-015/OQ-009/OQ-010.
- [SM-ORDER-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-order-001) owns each Shop order's `PAYMENT_PENDING`/`PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`/`PAYMENT_FAILED`/`PAYMENT_EXPIRED` payment-related outcomes and settlement-exception annotation. Payment success moves all group orders to paid/awaiting confirmation. Later Seller confirmation moves an individual Shop order to `CONFIRMED` in UC-013 and is not a new payment-success effect.
- [SM-RESERVATION-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-reservation-001) owns `ACTIVE_PAYMENT`/`ACTIVE_PAID` and terminal consumed/released/expired holds. Payment success converts all matching unpaid holds into paid holds with the same quantities and unchanged physical stock. Payment failure/expiry releases unpaid holds; it cannot release a paid hold. Seller confirmation later consumes only that Shop order's paid holds and deducts matching stock once. Paid timeout/cancellation/rejection/refund handling remains OQ-008/OQ-009/OQ-010.
- Preserve request/group/Shop-order identity, immutable event evidence, processing classification, provider outcome, purchase outcome, relevant times, and reservation/stock effects for audit and recovery. Identifier formats, callback fields/signature scheme, and persistence/transaction mechanisms belong to later design; this specification does not select them.
- Proposed payment audit coverage includes initiation/recovery, accepted outcome, rejection/conflict classification, reconciliation requirement, and linked paid-order/paid-hold/release effects. Records identify actor or simulated partner, action, changed data/outcome, timestamp, request/group and affected Shop orders/reservations. Do not include verification secrets. Retention, access, redaction, and rejected-attempt coverage remain OQ-005/OQ-012/OQ-015.

## Postconditions

- Successful payment: one `SUCCEEDED` outcome; all group Shop orders `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`; all matching holds `ACTIVE_PAID`; physical stock unchanged. Seller confirmation/stock deduction is pending separately per Shop order.
- Failure/expiry: all group Shop orders `PAYMENT_FAILED` or `PAYMENT_EXPIRED`; unpaid holds released once; no payment-driven stock deduction; no automatic second request or retry purchase.
- Cancellation/paid closure: the owning UC-015 unpaid-group outcome remains CANCELLED; authentic late success needs reconciliation. UC-013/015 paid-Shop closure preserves SUCCEEDED and adds a separate compensation obligation, not a refund-completed outcome.
- Reconciliation: verified evidence and the established purchase outcome are preserved separately, with an observable unresolved case; no automatic resurrection, oversell, refund, or reversal is claimed.
- Rejected/unauthorized action: protected payment, order, inventory, and reservation data remain unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

All criteria below are **Proposed** specification examples. They are not executed test evidence. Exact value fixtures, expiry duration, retry limits, and permission matrix remain linked open decisions.

- **AC-UC-012-01 — One request per group:** given an accepted two-Shop group with a recorded amount/currency, when its Buyer initiates payment repeatedly, the provider and Buyer reference one logical request for that group and the same recorded amount/currency; no second payable request is created.
- **AC-UC-012-02 — Purchase ownership:** given another Buyer's group/request, when an unauthorized Buyer or Shop operator requests initiation/status, no protected purchase/payment details are disclosed and no request, stock, reservation, or Shop order changes.
- **AC-UC-012-03 — Verified complete success:** given one pending group with all matching holds `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` before expiry, when authenticated success matches its request/amount/currency, one complete outcome moves payment to `SUCCEEDED`, all its Shop orders to `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`, and all holds to `ACTIVE_PAID`. Quantities remain reserved, physical stock is unchanged, and Seller confirmation is still pending separately per Shop.
- **AC-UC-012-04 — Invalid callback rejection:** malformed evidence, missing required identity, or invalid authentication cannot change payment application, Orders, holds or stock or create a reconciliation conflict solely from that claim. A new authenticated event with unknown/mismatched request or amount/currency is also rejected without those effects. Authenticated reuse of an already-known event identity with changed facts follows AC-UC-012-11 instead. Rejected diagnostics remain permission-scoped.
- **AC-UC-012-05 — Duplicate success:** given completed success, replay its exact event sequentially/concurrently, or deliver a new authenticated event ID with the same correlated successful outcome. Return established success/current Order progress with one effective paid transition and paid-hold conversion; preserve subsequent Seller confirmation or original deadline passing. Payment replay causes no deduction/confirmation and no missing-unpaid-hold error solely from completed progress. NFR-002's replay count remains a later fixture, not proof here.
- **AC-UC-012-06 — Failure releases all Shops:** given a pending multi-Shop group with active holds, when verified failure is applied, all group Shop orders close unconfirmed and all holds release once; physical stock is unchanged and no retry request is automatically created.
- **AC-UC-012-07 — Expiry without callback:** given a pending group whose authoritative expiry has arrived without a completed success, expiry processing moves payment to `EXPIRED`, all orders to `PAYMENT_EXPIRED`, and releases all unpaid holds once. Delayed cleanup does not allow another success to use an expired hold.
- **AC-UC-012-08 — Repeated failure/expiry:** given a group already closed unsuccessfully, repeated exact failure/expiry processing causes no additional release, deduction, Shop-order transition, or automatic new request.
- **AC-UC-012-09 — Late success:** given an expired group whose released quantity has become available to another checkout, when a verified success arrives for the original request, the original group receives no paid transition, confirmation, or stock deduction; provider success is preserved with reconciliation `REQUIRED` and no automatic refund is reported.
- **AC-UC-012-10 — Expiry race boundary:** given verified success and expiry processing competing for a pending group, success commits only if every hold is active strictly before expiry at the business outcome boundary. Otherwise the group closes unsuccessfully and any success evidence requires reconciliation; final quantities and all Shop orders reflect exactly one complete outcome.
- **AC-UC-012-11 — Event-ID conflict:** given previously recorded authenticated event identity/evidence, when authenticated evidence under that same provider-scoped ID carries changed request, amount/currency or outcome, comparison against the known record marks reconciliation REQUIRED for the original association before new-event correlation. Preserve the established application and quantity effects, do not apply changed financial facts, and do not expose a different Buyer's purchase. An unauthenticated reuse is rejected under AC-UC-012-04.
- **AC-UC-012-12 — Opposite terminal evidence:** given a completed success or unsuccessful terminal outcome, when authentic opposite terminal evidence is received, reconciliation is required and the established purchase outcome, quantities, and Shop-order states are preserved; no blind cancellation, restock, or resurrection occurs.
- **AC-UC-012-13 — Lost initiation response:** given that the provider created the logical request but its response was lost, when initiation is recovered, the same request identity/status is obtained; the Buyer is not given a second payable request and holds are not extended by recovery.
- **AC-UC-012-14 — Lost success response:** given that the group success business outcome completed but its response was lost, when the Buyer retries or the event is redelivered, the existing successful status is returned with no repeated paid-hold conversion, stock deduction, or Seller confirmation and no loss of later Seller progress.
- **AC-UC-012-15 — Recovery after receipt-only interruption:** given verified success evidence recorded before interruption but no group business outcome completed, when recovery occurs after any required unpaid hold expires, it records reconciliation `REQUIRED`, releases remaining unpaid holds once, and makes no paid transition, order confirmation, or stock deduction.
- **AC-UC-012-16 — Recovery before expiry:** given uncommitted verified success with all holds still active before expiry, recovery may apply the complete successful outcome once; a repeated recovery returns the recorded result without repeated effects. The recovery-time target remains Proposed in NFR-006/OQ-012.
- **AC-UC-012-17 — Missing group hold:** given otherwise matching verified success but one required unpaid reservation is missing, released, expired, or already consumed inconsistently, no partial paid transition occurs; reconciliation identifies the inconsistency without new stock deduction. Remaining `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` holds release only under applicable failure/expiry guards, not by blindly releasing unexpected `ACTIVE_PAID` holds.
- **AC-UC-012-18 — Separate evidence and purchase status:** given provider success that cannot fulfill an expired/failed/cancelled group, the authorized status view exposes both the recorded provider evidence/reconciliation requirement and the unsuccessful purchase result; it cannot describe the group as fulfilled or refunded solely from that callback.
- **AC-UC-012-19 — Snapshot correlation:** given an accepted group followed by permitted catalog price/cart changes, initiation and callback checks use its recorded purchase amount/currency and exact reserved quantities; current cart/catalog values cannot silently alter the request or replace an ordered SKU.
- **AC-UC-012-20 — Audit and attempt classification:** given initiation, terminal outcomes, duplicates, rejected evidence, conflicts, and recovered processing, authorized audit inspection can correlate each agreed record to the request/group/event and affected orders/reservations, distinguish delivery attempts from business changes, and identify actor/action/outcome/time without verification secrets.
- **AC-UC-012-21 — Buyer return or abandonment:** a Buyer-controlled return page or abandoned payment interaction alone cannot confirm, deduct, or mark verified payment failure; revisiting returns the same request/status while eligible and authoritative expiry still applies.
- **AC-UC-012-22 — Simulated boundary:** all initiation, callback, recovery, and reconciliation scenarios use the simulated provider; the demo performs no real-money charge or refund.
- **AC-UC-012-23 — Paid holds survive unpaid expiry:** given a complete successful payment before the deadline, when its original unpaid deadline or a duplicate expiry job occurs, payment stays `SUCCEEDED`, `ACTIVE_PAID` holds remain reserved, physical stock is unchanged, and Shop orders retain their paid or later valid Seller outcomes. No paid timeout/cancellation/release is inferred from the original unpaid-expiry job; separately accepted owning UC-013/015 closure keeps its own cause and effects.
- **AC-UC-012-24 — Seller confirmation remains separate:** given a paid two-Shop group, payment processing confirms neither Shop nor deducts stock; later confirmation of one Shop order under UC-013 consumes and deducts only that Shop's paid quantities once while the sibling remains paid/awaiting confirmation with its own active paid holds. Downstream cancellation/rejection criteria are Proposed in UC-013/015 under OQ-009; refund execution remains pending UC-017/OQ-010.

Criteria AC-UC-012-01/13/19 relate to BR-PAY-001/BR-ORDER-001; AC-UC-012-03/06 through 10/15 through 17/23/24 relate to all three Proposed rules and their three state models. AC-UC-012-04/05/11/12/14/18/21 relate to BR-PAY-001/NFR-002/NFR-006; AC-UC-012-02/20 relate to NFR-001/NFR-004. Analysis/design, acceptance test artifacts, and executed verification are pending.
