# UC-013 — Confirm and fulfill a Shop order

## Metadata

- Status: Draft; Seller-response timing, paid-order closure, compensation, packing, and handover behavior are Proposed under OQ-008/OQ-009/OQ-010/OQ-015. Baseline Seller confirmation remains separate from payment success.
- Owner: Project owner.
- Priority: P0 — Proposed in the [catalog](../USE_CASE_CATALOG.md).
- Scope/source: SC-013/SC-012, supporting SC-007/SC-024/SC-025/SC-026 in [Functional Scope](../../01-product/FUNCTIONAL_SCOPE.md); WI-004 continuation authorized on 2026-10-07. This authorizes Draft preparation, not acceptance of its product choices.

## Goal

Let an authorized Seller decide one paid Shop order, deduct its protected inventory only on confirmation, prepare all its accepted items, and request simulated delivery. Close an unconfirmed paid order safely when Seller rejects it, Buyer cancels it, or the Seller-response deadline expires, while keeping the financial obligation visible.

## Primary actor

Seller / Shop Operator with permission for the target Shop and action. Exact operator, owner, and Internal Staff rights remain [OQ-005](../OPEN_DECISIONS.md#oq-005).

## Supporting actors

- Buyer may cancel before Seller confirmation through [UC-015](UC-015-track-cancel-orders.md); its action competes with Seller confirmation/rejection and timeout.
- The system applies the authoritative Seller-response deadline and recovers interrupted actions.
- Simulated Payment Provider supplies the already-applied payment outcome through [UC-012](UC-012-process-payment.md). Compensation execution is future UC-017 work.
- Simulated Logistics Partner receives shipment requests and supplies pickup/tracking evidence through [UC-014](UC-014-simulate-shipment.md).

## Trigger

Seller opens a permitted paid Shop order and chooses confirmation or rejection; after confirmation, Seller records packing and requests shipment/handover. Independently, the Seller-response deadline may become due while the order remains unconfirmed.

## Preconditions

- Access and mutation require the relevant target-Shop permission. Access to this Shop order does not grant sibling-Shop records, the complete payment group, or unrelated Buyer data.
- The accepted purchase snapshot and stable Shop-order/group identities from [UC-011](UC-011-checkout.md) exist. Fulfillment uses its exact ordered SKU identities, quantities, descriptions, amounts, address, and shipping choice; current catalog/cart edits do not rewrite them.
- A **new confirmation** requires `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`, a fully applied group Payment Request outcome of `SUCCEEDED`, no unresolved payment reconciliation requirement, and every required exact hold in `ACTIVE_PAID` with consistent inventory totals.
- A **new confirmation or Seller rejection** must be accepted strictly before the recorded Seller-response deadline. Proposed [OQ-009](../OPEN_DECISIONS.md#oq-009) uses a per-Shop deadline, with **24 hours from applied group-payment success** as a reviewable candidate. Receipt/provider occurrence time, opening the order, replay, or a delayed timer cannot reset or extend it. At/after the deadline, an unconfirmed order follows the timeout closure rather than a new confirmation/rejection.
- An already-accepted action is recovered after authorization and identity checks, before testing new-action state, hold, or deadline guards. Recovery returns its established result and current progress; it is not another fulfillment action.
- Missing/inconsistent purchase facts, SKU associations, inventory effects, or required holds are explicit issues. Do not fabricate replacements, force counters, or bypass unresolved Shop/staff restrictions; correction authority remains OQ-004/OQ-005/OQ-006/OQ-015.

## Main flow

1. Seller requests the target Shop order. The system checks actor, Shop scope, and permitted action before displaying protected data or resolving an action result.
2. Seller sees the accepted order lines, quantities, snapshotted shipping information, applied payment status, current order state, and Seller-response deadline. Distinguish payment success, Seller acceptance, shipment progress, and any unresolved financial exception.
3. Seller submits confirmation with a stable logical action identity. Resolve a recorded result and immutable instructions first. An exact accepted repeat returns that result/current progress; changed instructions under the same identity are refused.
4. For a new confirmation, evaluate the current authoritative order/payment/hold/inventory/deadline guards together. Accept `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` → `CONFIRMED`, consume all this Shop order's exact `ACTIVE_PAID` holds, and deduct their quantities from both `on_hand` and `reserved` as one indivisible business outcome. No line succeeds alone; `available` remains unchanged by confirmation. Preserve sibling-Shop orders and holds.
5. Record the confirmation's actor, order/action identity, time, complete hold/stock effects, and established result. Seller and Buyer can subsequently observe Seller acceptance separately from the earlier payment result.
6. Seller prepares every ordered line and records packing for the confirmed order. With permitted access, coherent confirmation effects, and no unresolved payment reconciliation requirement, accept `CONFIRMED` → `PACKED` once. Packing neither deducts inventory again nor substitutes/drops items.
7. Seller requests one complete shipment for the packed Shop order through [UC-014](UC-014-simulate-shipment.md), retaining the accepted address, shipping choice, and all ordered quantities. Shipment creation/recovery uses that use case's guards and identity; partial or split shipments remain outside this Proposed choice.
8. Seller may record a permitted handover intent after a shipment is established and awaiting pickup. Preserve the request's result on repeat. Handover intent does not establish carrier pickup, `SHIPPED`, or delivery; those outcomes require admissible simulated Logistics Partner evidence in UC-014.

## Alternative and error flows

- **A1 — Unauthorized/wrong-Shop action:** refuse protected access or mutation. A User with rights to another Shop, Buyer-only rights, or an unspecified Internal Staff role gains no automatic fulfillment permission. Current authorization is checked even for replay/recovery.
- **A2 — Unpaid, unsuccessful, or financially uncertain order:** a new confirmation requires applied `SUCCEEDED`, not a Buyer return-page claim, unverified callback, or merely received provider evidence. `PAYMENT_PENDING`, `PAYMENT_FAILED`, `PAYMENT_EXPIRED`, or payment reconciliation `REQUIRED` blocks new confirmation. An unresolved payment reconciliation requirement also blocks new packing, shipment creation, and handover. Preserve already-applied effects and show the issue; do not refund, cancel, restock, or resolve reconciliation by inference.
- **A3 — Missing/inconsistent paid holds:** if any required hold is absent, released, consumed inconsistently, belongs to another Shop/SKU/order, or has the wrong quantity, refuse new confirmation without partial consumption/deduction. Record an observable consistency issue for authorized review. An expected `CONSUMED` hold of an already-confirmed order is not an error solely because an accepted confirmation is replayed.
- **A4 — Seller rejection before deadline:** Seller supplies a recorded nonempty reason for an unconfirmed paid Shop order. Check permission, identity, current state, applied payment, exact releasable paid holds, and acceptance strictly before the deadline. Apply `SELLER_REJECTED`, release all this order's paid holds once, and record its stable compensation obligation together. Missing/invalid reason causes no closure or quantity effect. Confirmed/packed/shipped orders are outside this rejection path.
- **A5 — Buyer cancellation before confirmation:** UC-015 owns Buyer access and cancellation intent. Before the deadline, its accepted closure competes with Seller actions using the same current state/hold guards. A winning cancellation records `CANCELLED`, releases this Shop order's paid holds, and creates the same required compensation obligation; Seller can no longer confirm it. Unpaid group cancellation is a separate Proposed UC-015 path; cancellation after confirmation remains unresolved.
- **A6 — Seller-response timeout:** at/after the recorded deadline, new confirmation/rejection/Buyer paid-cancellation is ineligible even if a worker has not run. Expose timeout-due status and route the current unconfirmed order to guarded timeout. With applied `SUCCEEDED` and exact `ACTIVE_PAID` holds, accept `SELLER_TIMED_OUT`, paid-hold release, and required compensation together. An unresolved payment reconciliation annotation does not indefinitely retain otherwise valid paid stock: preserve/link its financial exception alongside the compensation obligation. Missing/inconsistent facts still require an explicit recovery issue rather than guessed release.
- **A7 — Paid closure compensation:** A4/A5/A6 create one stable obligation for the closing Shop order's snapshotted payable amount/currency, including its attributed shipping and discounts. Mark compensation `REQUIRED`, awaiting the separate UC-017 resolution/execution specification. Physical stock is unchanged; reserved decreases and available increases. Pending compensation does not keep these paid holds reserved in this Proposed choice. Group payment stays `SUCCEEDED`; sibling orders/holds keep their own progress. Do not recompute catalog prices, redistribute sibling charges/discounts, or describe a refund as completed.
- **A8 — Closure cannot record all effects:** if the order transition, every eligible paid-hold release, stable compensation obligation, or required outcome evidence cannot be recorded together, none of those closure effects is accepted. Preserve the paid outcome/holds and expose the incomplete attempt for recovery. A missing compensation amount/allocation is not silently treated as zero.
- **A9 — Competing decisions:** confirmation, rejection, cancellation, and timeout compete against the same authoritative current state and eligible time. One complete accepted outcome wins. Confirmation consumes/deducts; closure releases/records compensation without deduction. A losing new action returns the current outcome without opposite effects. At the deadline, timeout is the eligible closure; an earlier-received but uncommitted confirmation/rejection/cancellation cannot override that boundary.
- **A10 — Repeat, conflicting identity, or lost response:** after authorization, return an exact accepted operation's original result/current progress before new-action guards, including after packing, shipment progress, or deadline passage. No repeated consumption, release, obligation, packing, handover intent, effective notification intent, or business audit change occurs. Reuse of the identity with different immutable instructions is refused. A deliberately new action needs its own identity and current guards.
- **A11 — Interrupted uncommitted action:** recover the same action and determine whether its complete outcome already exists. If not, re-evaluate current state, time, holds, and payment; receipt alone establishes neither confirmation nor closure and does not stop the clock. A confirmation interrupted until after the deadline cannot be freshly accepted. No partial stock/order/obligation outcome is reported as valid success; technical recovery mechanisms remain later design work.
- **A12 — Packing incomplete or already progressed:** packing requires all accepted lines. Missing quantity, a substitute SKU, partial preparation, an unconfirmed/closed order, or inconsistent recorded confirmation effects cannot produce `PACKED`. An accepted packing replay returns current progress, including later shipment states, without regression. A newly identified pack action after `PACKED` or later progress cannot repack/deduct the order.
- **A13 — Product changes, restrictions, or SKU identity problems:** later Product hiding alone does not undo the accepted approved snapshot or create a cancellation/rejection/refund. Fulfill the recorded exact identities; no new catalog content or substitute SKU is silently used. Staff/Shop restrictions and destructive SKU reidentification remain open dependencies; flag affected actions for authorized resolution rather than granting a bypass.
- **A14 — Post-confirmation failure or cancellation request:** confirmation has already consumed/deducted inventory. The pre-confirmation reject/cancel/timeout path cannot release consumed holds or restore stock. Record/route the delivery or after-sales issue through UC-014/015 and pending UC-016/017 policy under OQ-010; no automatic restock or refund follows from packing failure, handover intent, a delivery exception, or a refund claim.
- **A15 — Shipment creation/handover unavailable:** keep the packed order and recover the same shipment/request through UC-014. An unknown creation or handover result is not pickup; never create a second active shipment or label the order `SHIPPED` merely to bypass uncertainty. No additional stock deduction occurs.

## State/data changes

- [SM-ORDER-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-order-001) owns this Shop order's `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` → `CONFIRMED` → `PACKED`, and pre-confirmation terminal `SELLER_REJECTED`/`SELLER_TIMED_OUT`/`CANCELLED` outcomes. Record the authoritative response deadline and decision/packing identities; neither payment success nor handover intent implies `SHIPPED`.
- [SM-RESERVATION-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-reservation-001) and [SM-INVENTORY-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-inventory-001) own `ACTIVE_PAID` → `CONSUMED` with equal on_hand/reserved deduction, or `ACTIVE_PAID` → `RELEASED` with no on_hand deduction. Each Shop order's complete line effects occur together; terminal holds cannot be reused.
- [SM-PAYMENT-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-payment-001) retains the successful group application and separate reconciliation evidence. A Shop closure creates its linked compensation obligation `REQUIRED`; this annotation is neither a failed group payment nor a completed refund. UC-017 compensation execution/closure remains pending.
- [SM-SHIPMENT-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-shipment-001) and UC-014 own shipment creation, awaiting pickup, actual pickup/tracking, and delivery exceptions. One complete shipment per Shop order is the Proposed working boundary; a handover intent is recorded evidence of a request, not carrier evidence.
- Proposed audit coverage links actor or timeout system action, target Shop/order, logical action identity, state before/after, authoritative time/deadline, rejection reason, hold/stock before/after, payment identity, and any compensation obligation/amount/currency. Distinguish attempts and recovery from effective changes. Protect Buyer address/payment details; fields, retention, redaction, rejection-attempt coverage, and access are OQ-005/OQ-012 design dependencies.

## Postconditions

- Confirmed: all this order's paid holds consumed; on_hand/reserved deducted once; available unchanged; Seller acceptance recorded; siblings unchanged.
- Packed: every accepted line prepared and packing recorded; no new inventory deduction or carrier pickup claim.
- Closed before confirmation: permitted terminal reason, all its paid holds released once, no physical deduction, and one required compensation obligation for its accepted Shop payable amount. Pending financial resolution is visible; siblings continue independently.
- Refused/failed attempt: no partial accepted stock, order, shipment, or compensation effect; an inconsistency or unknown result remains observable for recovery.
- Shipment/handover requested: UC-014 retains shipment progress separately; real payment/logistics integration remains outside the learning demo.

## Acceptance criteria

All criteria below are **Proposed** specification scenarios. They are not executed tests or product acceptance. The 24-hour candidate, monetary/unit fixtures, permissions, recovery/audit targets, and after-sales effects remain open decisions.

- **AC-UC-013-01 — Shop/action authorization:** given an order of Shop A, when a Shop B operator, Buyer-only actor, or actor without the required Shop A action permission reads/confirms/rejects/packs/requests handover, protected details are not disclosed and no order, stock, hold, shipment, or obligation changes. Authorization also precedes accepted-action retrieval.
- **AC-UC-013-02 — Applied payment prerequisite:** given a pending/failed/expired payment, only received/unverified success evidence, or a `REQUIRED` payment reconciliation annotation, a new confirmation is refused without consumption/deduction. Existing applied outcomes/evidence remain visible to permitted actors.
- **AC-UC-013-03 — Complete confirmation effect:** given a permitted paid Shop order before its deadline with exact active paid holds for all its lines, confirmation moves it to `CONFIRMED` and every hold to `CONSUMED` together. For each required quantity q, on_hand and reserved each decrease by q, available stays unchanged, and neither counter becomes negative.
- **AC-UC-013-04 — Sibling independence:** given one successful two-Shop payment group, confirmation of Shop A changes only A's order/holds/stock. Shop B remains at its own current state with its holds unchanged, and A's operator gains no sibling private details.
- **AC-UC-013-05 — Exact hold consistency:** given one missing, wrong-quantity, wrong-SKU/order, released, or inconsistently consumed hold among several required lines, new confirmation accepts no line/stock effect, flags the issue, and cannot acquire replacement stock or silently substitute another SKU.
- **AC-UC-013-06 — Response deadline source:** given applied group-payment success at time t, each Shop order records the candidate deadline t + 24 hours only as the configured Proposed fixture. Callback occurrence/receipt, order opening, repeat actions, or the original unpaid-expiry job cannot extend that deadline or close an already-confirmed order.
- **AC-UC-013-07 — Deadline boundary:** given an unconfirmed paid order and delayed timeout processing, a new confirm/reject/Buyer paid-cancel accepted at or after its deadline cannot win that action. The system exposes/routes timeout-due closure. Before the deadline an otherwise eligible action may win; a claimed earlier request time alone does not establish acceptance.
- **AC-UC-013-08 — Rejection and reason:** given all eligible paid holds before the deadline, authorized Seller rejection with a valid recorded reason applies `SELLER_REJECTED`, releases every paid hold once without physical deduction, and records one required compensation obligation together. An absent/invalid reason or already-confirmed order yields none of those effects.
- **AC-UC-013-09 — Paid timeout:** given an unconfirmed `SUCCEEDED` paid order with exact `ACTIVE_PAID` holds at/after its deadline, timeout applies `SELLER_TIMED_OUT`, releases them once without physical deduction, and records the required compensation obligation. An existing payment reconciliation requirement is preserved/linked and does not indefinitely prevent this otherwise valid timeout closure.
- **AC-UC-013-10 — Compensation amount and visibility:** given a paid closure, its stable obligation references the closing Shop order's snapshotted payable amount/currency, including attributed shipping/discounts, and remains `REQUIRED` pending UC-017. Stock is available after release even while compensation is pending; no completed refund is displayed and current catalog values cannot recompute the obligation.
- **AC-UC-013-11 — Closure failure and sibling preservation:** given inability to record any required closure transition, hold release, or obligation/evidence, no partial closure/release is accepted. A successful later recovery creates only the closing Shop's effects; sibling progress/holds and the group `SUCCEEDED` application remain unchanged.
- **AC-UC-013-12 — Competing decisions:** given concurrent confirm/reject/Buyer paid-cancel/timeout for one order, authoritative current state/time admits one complete outcome. A confirmation deducts/consumes once; a closing action releases once with compensation and no deduction. The losing action causes no opposite stock/obligation effect; at the deadline the eligible new closure is timeout.
- **AC-UC-013-13 — Accepted-action recovery first:** given an accepted confirmation/rejection/packing/handover action whose response was lost, an authorized exact repeat after later order progress, hold consumption/release, reconciliation change, or deadline passage returns its established result/current progress before new-action guards. No quantity effect, obligation, notification intent, or effective business audit change is repeated and later progress is preserved.
- **AC-UC-013-14 — Immutable action identity:** given an action identity with recorded immutable instructions, reuse with a changed target, action, or rejection reason is refused without altered effects. A deliberately distinct action evaluates fresh current permission/state/time guards rather than inheriting an earlier successful result.
- **AC-UC-013-15 — Uncommitted recovery:** given a received confirmation interrupted before complete acceptance, recovery uses its original identity and current guards. If the deadline has since arrived, it cannot newly confirm/deduct; it routes timeout handling. Recovery cannot present partial order/stock/obligation effects as a completed result.
- **AC-UC-013-16 — Complete packing once:** given coherent `CONFIRMED` effects and permitted access without unresolved payment reconciliation, packing all accepted lines changes the order to `PACKED` once with no additional deduction. Partial quantities, substitution, closed/unconfirmed orders, or inconsistent confirmation effects cannot produce `PACKED`; replay preserves later shipment progress.
- **AC-UC-013-17 — Fulfillment exception block:** given a confirmed or packed order followed by payment reconciliation `REQUIRED`, new packing, shipment creation, or handover is blocked with an explicit financial issue. Prior accepted progress and stock deductions remain; an authorized replay retrieves its existing result without introducing a new fulfillment effect.
- **AC-UC-013-18 — Shipment and handover boundary:** given a packed complete order, shipment creation/recovery delegates to UC-014 for one complete shipment using accepted quantities/address/shipping. Handover intent or an unknown request result alone never produces pickup/`SHIPPED`, a second active shipment, or another deduction; admissible simulated partner evidence establishes actual progress.
- **AC-UC-013-19 — Snapshot and later hiding:** given a paid accepted order followed by Product hiding or permitted catalog/cart/address edits, fulfillment retains its accepted approved descriptions, exact identities/quantities, payable facts, and shipping snapshot. Hiding alone cannot cancel/refund/restock it. Unresolved restriction or SKU identity problems produce an explicit issue rather than an invented bypass/substitution.
- **AC-UC-013-20 — No pre-confirmation closure after confirmation:** given consumed holds and a confirmed/packed/shipped order, the pre-confirmation reject/cancel/timeout path cannot release them, undo physical deduction, or mark a refund completed. Delivery/after-sales issues follow linked policy work; a refund claim or logistics exception alone never restores inventory.
- **AC-UC-013-21 — Auditable complete outcomes:** given confirmation, rejection, timeout, packing, handover, repeat, and interrupted-action recovery, permitted inspection correlates each agreed record with Shop/order/action identity, actor/action/time, prior/current state and any hold/stock or obligation amount effects. Rejection retains its reason; attempts are distinguishable from effective changes and unauthorized Buyer/sibling data remains protected.
- **AC-UC-013-22 — Separate user-visible outcomes:** given paid awaiting confirmation, confirmed, packed, handover requested, or closed with required compensation, permitted Seller/Buyer views distinguish those outcomes from payment success, verified pickup/delivery, and completed refund. All partner/compensation scenarios use simulation without real money or real carrier execution.

Criteria AC-UC-013-02 through 07/12 through 17 relate to BR-ORDER-002/BR-INV-001, SM-ORDER-001/SM-RESERVATION-001/SM-INVENTORY-001 and NFR-003/006; AC-UC-013-08 through 12/20 relate to BR-CANCEL-001/BR-ORDER-001/BR-PAY-001 and the Order/reservation/payment models. AC-UC-013-18/22 connect to UC-014/015 and SM-SHIPMENT-001; AC-UC-013-19 preserves BR-PROD-002/004 and purchase snapshots. AC-UC-013-01/21 relate to NFR-001/004.
