# UC-015 — Track, cancel, and complete a purchase

## Metadata

- Status: Draft specification; cancellation scope, deadlines, compensation obligations, and completion lifecycle behavior are **Proposed**, pending OQ-007/OQ-008/OQ-009/OQ-010/OQ-015. Buyer completion and auto-completion are drafted under BR-AFTERSALES-001.
- Owner: Project owner for product choices; analyst/assistant for specification.
- Scope: SC-007; connected checkout/payment, stock, fulfillment, audit, after-sales and tracking dependencies SC-005/SC-006/SC-008/SC-012/SC-013/SC-020/SC-022/SC-025/SC-026 in [Functional Scope](../../01-product/FUNCTIONAL_SCOPE.md).
- Source: [catalog](../USE_CASE_CATALOG.md), [UC-011](UC-011-checkout.md), [UC-012](UC-012-process-payment.md), connected [UC-013](UC-013-fulfill-shop-order.md)/[UC-014](UC-014-simulate-shipment.md), [UC-016](UC-016-request-return-refund.md), and the owner's 2026-10-07 WI-005 continuation request. All payment/logistics actions remain simulated.
- Priority: P0 — Proposed in the [catalog](../USE_CASE_CATALOG.md); confirmation remains OQ-014.

## Goal

Let Buyer observe the outcome of each Shop order in an owned purchase group, recover an interrupted cancellation without duplicate effects, explicitly cancel an eligible unpaid group or one eligible paid Shop order, and confirm delivery to complete the purchase. Provide system auto-completion when the delivery confirmation window elapses without dispute. Keep payment application, financial evidence, compensation, Shop fulfillment, shipment progress, and after-sales dispute states distinguishable.

## Primary actor

Buyer acting through the User account that owns the purchase group and its Shop orders. Account prerequisites remain OQ-005.

## Supporting actors

- Seller / Shop Operator confirms, rejects, packs or hands over its permitted Shop order through [UC-013](UC-013-fulfill-shop-order.md); Seller permission does not grant Buyer access to the full private group or its siblings.
- The system applies authoritative payment/Seller-response deadlines, resolves competing actions, and exposes existing cancellation/recovery outcomes.
- Simulated Payment Provider and Logistics Partner supply verified evidence through [UC-012](UC-012-process-payment.md)/[UC-014](UC-014-simulate-shipment.md). A Buyer-controlled page or cancellation request is not partner-payment evidence.
- Authorized Internal Staff may inspect correlated financial/fulfillment exceptions. Actual compensation decisions/execution and intervention permissions remain UC-017/OQ-005/OQ-010/OQ-015; tracking or cancelling does not grant staff authority to Buyer.

## Trigger

Buyer opens or refreshes an owned purchase, explicitly requests cancellation with its intended scope, or recovers the response to the same cancellation operation.

## Preconditions

- The accepted purchase group, per-Shop orders, immutable purchase snapshot, exact Shop/SKU associations and recorded payment identity exist from UC-011/012. Current cart/catalog changes do not replace purchased facts.
- Reading or cancellation recovery checks current Buyer ownership and operation association before disclosure. Having Shop operating rights alone is insufficient.
- A new cancellation has a stable logical identity and immutable instructions identifying either **the entire unpaid purchase group** or **one named paid Shop order**, including any required reason. A repeat resolves an already-recorded result before new-action state/time guards; changed instructions under the same identity are refused.
- Unpaid cancellation requires payment application `PENDING`, every group Shop order `PAYMENT_PENDING`, and every exact group hold `ACTIVE_PAYMENT`, with acceptance strictly before the recorded unpaid deadline. It cannot silently select one unpaid Shop or reinterpret a paid group.
- Paid cancellation requires the named Shop order `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION`, payment application `SUCCEEDED`, all its exact matching holds `ACTIVE_PAID`, and acceptance strictly before its recorded Seller-response deadline. The response deadline is established by UC-012/013 under Proposed OQ-008/OQ-009; refreshing the view does not extend it. The 24-hour candidate remains unconfirmed in [OQ-009](../OPEN_DECISIONS.md#oq-009).
- Paid closure also requires recording a stable compensation obligation for that Shop order's accepted payable amount/currency. Financial reconciliation `REQUIRED` alone does not prove failed payment or prevent otherwise coherent closure: preserve and correlate that exception with the obligation for authorized disposition. Inconsistent application/hold/amount associations cannot be bypassed by cancellation. No refund completion is implied.

## Main flow

All new policy in this flow is Proposed; canonical definitions belong to the linked rules/states/decisions.

1. Buyer opens an owned purchase group or Shop order. The system verifies ownership and returns stable group/Shop-order identities, accepted item quantities/prices, delivery facts, attributed shipping/discounts, per-Shop payable amounts and currency from the original snapshot.
2. The view presents each Shop order's current fulfillment outcome separately from the group's payment-application outcome, relevant verified financial evidence/reconciliation, any per-Shop compensation obligation, and correlated shipment progress/exception. A paid group can contain a cancelled Shop and a sibling continuing to fulfillment.
3. The system presents the applicable recorded deadlines and the cancellation scope currently eligible under the canonical guards. A stale view is informational; it does not authorize a later change. Authoritative overdue outcomes are recovered through their owning UC-012/013 flow rather than labelled still eligible merely because a worker is delayed.
4. Buyer selects an offered cancellation scope and explicitly submits the logical operation with those instructions. An unpaid choice covers the entire group; a paid choice names one Shop order. Submission does not turn `DELIVERED` into completed or initiate a return/refund case implicitly.
5. The system rechecks ownership and resolves that operation identity. Return an existing outcome and current progress for an exact repeat; refuse changed instructions. For a genuinely new/uncommitted cancellation, recheck authoritative state/time, payment application, matching hold set, scope and required compensation facts.
6. **Eligible unpaid group:** record one complete business outcome moving application payment `PENDING` → `CANCELLED`, every group Shop order `PAYMENT_PENDING` → `CANCELLED`, and every matching unpaid hold `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` → `RELEASED`. Reserved quantities decrease once; physical stock is unchanged. No paid-refund entitlement is created solely by this cancellation.
7. **Eligible paid Shop order:** record one complete business outcome moving only that Shop order `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` → `CANCELLED`, releasing all its matching `ACTIVE_PAID` holds, and recording its stable compensation obligation as `REQUIRED` with Buyer-cancellation reason. The obligation covers the entire accepted Shop payable amount, including attributed shipping and discounts; it does not recalculate current fees/vouchers or execute a refund. If the complete obligation cannot be recorded, neither closure nor release succeeds.
8. For paid cancellation, group payment remains `SUCCEEDED`; sibling Shop orders, paid holds, shipments and payable allocations retain their current outcomes. The compensation obligation is correlated to any existing financial exception so later disposition cannot pay it twice. No second charge or full-group refund is introduced.
9. The system records attributable effective cancellation/stock/obligation changes and returns the original operation identity and outcome with current purchase progress. Buyer can revisit or recover a lost response using the same identity; repeats add no effective cancellation, release, compensation, business audit change or notification.
10. Buyer may continue tracking unaffected siblings through UC-014.
11. **Explicit Buyer completion:** when a Shop order is in `DELIVERED` status and has no active dispute case, Buyer may submit an explicit "Confirm Receipt" action with a stable operation identity. The system verifies ownership, validates absence of active dispute under BR-AFTERSALES-001, and transitions the Shop order `DELIVERED` → `COMPLETED`. Record completion time and Buyer actor. Regular after-sales return window closes.
12. **System auto-completion timeout:** if a Shop order remains in `DELIVERED` for the auto-completion window (7 days candidate under BR-AFTERSALES-001/OQ-010) without an open dispute case, the system transitions the Shop order `DELIVERED` → `COMPLETED` with system auto-completion timeout cause.

## Alternative and error flows

- **A1 — Unauthorized view, cancellation or recovery:** refuse without revealing another Buyer's protected group/address/payment/order details or changing any orders, quantities or obligations. A Seller may access only its separately permitted Shop-order view, not use Buyer tracking to inspect sibling private records.
- **A2 — Wrong scope or changed instructions:** a one-Shop unpaid cancellation is refused; one group-level paid instruction is not silently expanded into separate paid cancellations. Reuse of an operation identity with a different group/Shop/reason is refused; explicit new instructions need a distinct operation and current guards.
- **A3 — Unpaid deadline reached:** at or after the authoritative unpaid boundary, cancellation cannot relabel the group `CANCELLED` or produce a cancellation-based hold release. Recover the owning UC-012 expiry outcome: application `EXPIRED`, group orders `PAYMENT_EXPIRED` and unpaid holds `EXPIRED`, even if expiry processing was delayed. The view exposes the actual recovered outcome.
- **A4 — Unpaid cancellation versus applied payment success:** only one complete outcome can win through authoritative guards. Cancellation first closes/releases the unpaid group; later verified success preserves financial evidence and requires UC-012 reconciliation, without reopening orders/holds. Applied success first makes the unpaid cancellation ineligible; return its current paid status and require Buyer's explicit new instruction naming a Shop if paid cancellation remains eligible. Never silently reinterpret the original unpaid-group intent.
- **A5 — Failure, expiry or missing unpaid hold:** an already unsuccessful group cannot be cancelled as though pending. Missing, released, expired or mismatched required holds prevent a partial group cancellation; expose the inconsistency for authorized recovery. Existing state is not overwritten and no unexpected paid hold is blindly released.
- **A6 — Paid cancellation versus Seller confirmation/rejection:** evaluate new actions against the same authoritative paid-order outcome and deadline. One eligible accepted action wins; confirmation consumes/deducts its holds and makes ordinary Buyer cancellation unavailable. Buyer cancellation or permitted Seller rejection closes/releases only once with one stable compensation obligation and the actual accepted reason. The losing new action returns a conflict/current outcome without its own stock or payment effect.
- **A7 — Seller-response timeout:** at or after the recorded response deadline, new confirmation/rejection/Buyer cancellation are ineligible even if the timeout worker has not run. The owning UC-013 timeout may close the still-awaiting Shop order and release paid holds only with its stable required compensation obligation. Do not label this as Buyer cancellation. Recovery of an earlier completed Buyer cancellation still returns its original outcome after the cutoff.
- **A8 — Cannot record compensation or paid associations are inconsistent:** leave the Shop order and its paid holds unchanged; no closure, partial release, or claim of completed refund. Preserve diagnosable failure under the same operation identity and permit recovery only with current guards. A financial reconciliation flag alone is not a reason to discard verified success or force physical stock to stay held after an otherwise valid complete closure.
- **A9 — Already confirmed or later progress:** `CONFIRMED`, `PACKED`, `SHIPPED`, `DELIVERED` and subsequent delivery/after-sales outcomes have no ordinary Buyer cancellation transition in this proposal. Decline a genuinely new cancellation without replenishing already-consumed stock. Any later intervention/return/refund route depends on its own UC-016/017/OQ-010 policy.
- **A10 — Lost response or interrupted cancellation:** recover the same operation after ownership/instruction checks. A recorded complete cancellation returns its original outcome plus current progress; an uncommitted cancellation rechecks current state/time. Recovery cannot leave a closed paid Shop without its obligation, release a subset, double compensate or extend a deadline. Diagnostic attempts are distinct from effective changes.
- **A11 — Shipment exception or financial ambiguity:** tracking may show `DELIVERY_FAILED`, `RETURNING`, `RETURNED`, an unknown partner result or a reconciliation requirement alongside the Shop order/payment outcome. Verified failure/return-in-progress may retain order `SHIPPED` with an exception; verified return may project `DELIVERY_EXCEPTION` under UC-014. Deferred events with a missing predecessor remain processing gaps, not public physical progress. Such evidence alone does not cancel the purchase, mark compensation completed, or restore physical inventory. Current permitted projection/recovery follows UC-014; stock receipt and financial disposition remain separate work.
- **A12 — Delivered versus completed:** show delivery evidence as delivered, with active countdown to auto-completion window. Delivered alone does not immediately complete the order. An ordinary cancellation is not permitted.
- **A13 — Changed purchase content or unresolved voucher policy:** use the accepted snapshot for tracking and compensation allocation. Later content hiding, SKU changes, prices or voucher changes cannot rewrite the existing payable amount or recreate released holds. Voucher reissuance/quota/funding and unusual allocation corrections remain OQ-011; no current-policy guess changes cancellation accounting.
- **A14 — Completion blocked by active dispute:** if Buyer opens an after-sales dispute case under UC-016 while `DELIVERED`, auto-completion is suspended and explicit Buyer completion is blocked until the dispute is resolved or withdrawn.
- **A15 — Repeat completion:** sequential or concurrent replay of completion for an already `COMPLETED` order returns the existing completed outcome without repeated audit or state side-effects.

## State/data changes

- [SM-ORDER-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-order-001): unpaid cancellation closes every pending Shop order together; paid cancellation closes only the named awaiting-confirmation Shop order. Record the actual closure cause, actor, operation identity and deadline evaluation. Cancellation cannot overwrite later fulfillment, payment failure/expiry or a different already-accepted closure.
- [SM-PAYMENT-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-payment-001): unpaid application can become `CANCELLED`; paid cancellation preserves group `SUCCEEDED`. Verified financial evidence and reconciliation remain independent. A per-Shop compensation obligation `REQUIRED` is a pending obligation, not provider refund-success evidence or a new group-payment state; execution/closure remain UC-017/OQ-010/OQ-015.
- [SM-RESERVATION-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-reservation-001) and [SM-INVENTORY-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-inventory-001): eligible cancellation moves all owning active holds to `RELEASED` once, decreases reserved and increases available by those quantities, leaving on_hand unchanged. `CONSUMED` holds cannot be released through this ordinary cancellation path. Stock is not kept held merely because an already-recorded compensation obligation awaits execution.
- [SM-SHIPMENT-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-shipment-001): expose only the correlated Shop-order shipment and its current progress/exception; no Buyer cancellation mutates a sibling shipment. Completion, physical return receipt and after-sales models remain pending OQ-010.
- Preserve accepted group/Shop/SKU/item quantities and amounts/currency, attributed shipping/discounts, cancellation scope/reason, stable operation/obligation identities, effective time and before/after outcomes. Identifier format, persistence/concurrency and response contracts belong to later design.

## Postconditions

- Read/recovery: authorized Buyer receives existing purchase facts and distinguishable current outcomes; no new purchase, payment, shipment, deadline or inventory effect occurs solely from viewing.
- Successful unpaid cancellation: payment application and all group Shop orders are `CANCELLED`; all matching unpaid holds are `RELEASED`; physical stock is unchanged; no paid compensation is claimed solely from unpaid cancellation. Later financial success follows UC-012 reconciliation.
- Successful paid cancellation: one Shop order is `CANCELLED`, its matching paid holds are `RELEASED`, and its exact snapshotted payable compensation obligation is `REQUIRED`; payment application stays `SUCCEEDED`, sibling progress is preserved, and no simulated refund execution is claimed.
- Refused/new-ineligible cancellation: no cancellation-specific stock, order, payment or compensation change. An authoritative expiry/timeout recovered through UC-012/013 retains its own cause and linked outcome.
- Delivered/completed and after-sales eligibility remain separate; this Draft provides no automatic completion, returned-stock restoration or refund closure.

## Acceptance criteria

All criteria are **Proposed** specification scenarios, not executed tests. Use a two-Shop group, two distinct Buyers and exact SKU quantities; timing, shipping/discount fixtures, permissions and financial exception disposition remain linked open decisions.

- **AC-UC-015-01 — Owned purchase and snapshot:** given an owned accepted two-Shop group followed by cart/catalog changes, when Buyer opens it, the view identifies each Shop order and the original accepted quantities/prices, address/shipping facts, attributed discounts, payable amounts and currency; no current value silently replaces purchased facts.
- **AC-UC-015-02 — Private Buyer access:** given another Buyer's group/order/operation, an unauthorized Buyer cannot view, cancel or recover it; no protected purchase/address/payment facts are disclosed and no quantity, state or obligation changes.
- **AC-UC-015-03 — Seller scope:** given rights to one participating Shop only, its operator cannot use Buyer tracking/cancellation/recovery to inspect or change the private group or sibling Shop data. A separately permitted Seller view exposes only its own Shop-order scope.
- **AC-UC-015-04 — Distinguishable outcomes:** given group payment `SUCCEEDED`, one cancelled Shop with compensation `REQUIRED`, and a sibling in fulfillment, Buyer sees payment application, Shop outcomes, compensation and correlated shipment progress separately. A late-payment exception likewise shows financial evidence/reconciliation separately from an unsuccessful purchase; neither view claims completed refund solely from `REQUIRED`.
- **AC-UC-015-05 — Complete unpaid cancellation:** given payment `PENDING`, every group order `PAYMENT_PENDING` and all matching holds `ACTIVE_PAYMENT` strictly before expiry, explicit whole-group cancellation produces exactly one payment `CANCELLED`, all orders `CANCELLED`, and every hold `RELEASED`; reserved decreases once, on_hand is unchanged and no paid compensation entitlement is created solely by this action.
- **AC-UC-015-06 — Explicit scope and identity:** a one-Shop unpaid cancellation or a group-level paid cancellation is refused without silent scope conversion. Reusing a recorded operation identity with changed group/Shop/reason is refused without changing the original outcome or revealing a different purchase.
- **AC-UC-015-07 — Unpaid expiry wins eligibility:** given the recorded unpaid deadline has arrived but cleanup is delayed, a new cancellation cannot relabel the group cancelled or release it with Buyer-cancellation cause; UC-012 expiry yields payment `EXPIRED`, orders `PAYMENT_EXPIRED` and holds `EXPIRED`, with one release and unchanged physical stock.
- **AC-UC-015-08 — Payment-success race:** given unpaid cancellation and valid success competing before expiry, exactly one complete outcome applies. Success first refuses the unpaid intent and requires an explicit new paid-Shop instruction; cancellation first preserves cancelled orders/released holds when success later arrives, retaining verified evidence and reconciliation `REQUIRED` without resurrection or completed refund.
- **AC-UC-015-09 — Incomplete unpaid set:** given one group order or required hold no longer matches the pending unpaid set, a new cancellation cannot close/release only the remaining subset, overwrite the established outcome or release an unexpected paid hold; the inconsistency remains observable for authorized recovery.
- **AC-UC-015-10 — Paid per-Shop closure:** given one named `PAID_AWAITING_CONFIRMATION` order, applied payment `SUCCEEDED`, all its matching holds `ACTIVE_PAID` and time strictly before its response deadline, paid cancellation closes that Shop order, releases all its holds once, and records one stable compensation obligation `REQUIRED` in the same complete outcome; on_hand is unchanged.
- **AC-UC-015-11 — Exact compensation allocation:** given an accepted Shop payable amount with attributed shipping/discounts, paid cancellation records that amount/currency and Buyer-cancellation reason under one obligation identity. Changed current fees/prices/vouchers cannot alter the obligation; no new charge, provider refund-success claim or full-group refund is produced. If both eligible Shops are deliberately cancelled through separate operations, each has one attributed obligation and their sum is at most the original group payable amount; retries or competing reasons add no second entitlement. Actual execution caps against verified paid funds and prior compensation remain UC-017 work.
- **AC-UC-015-12 — Preserve paid siblings:** given a paid two-Shop group with one Shop awaiting confirmation and the other already progressing, cancellation of the eligible named Shop preserves group `SUCCEEDED`, the sibling's order/hold/shipment outcomes and original allocations; only the cancelled Shop's reserved quantity is released and compensation is required for that Shop alone.
- **AC-UC-015-13 — Compensation recording failure:** given otherwise eligible paid cancellation, when the complete stable obligation cannot be recorded, the Shop remains awaiting confirmation with every paid hold unchanged; no cancelled state, partial release or completed-refund claim is returned. Recovery uses the original operation and current guards.
- **AC-UC-015-14 — Reconciliation and inconsistent paid guards:** given applied payment `SUCCEEDED` with reconciliation `REQUIRED` but coherent matching paid holds/amounts, eligible cancellation may close/release with one correlated obligation while preserving the financial exception for authorized disposition. If any required paid association/hold is missing, released, consumed or mismatched instead, no partial cancellation/release/obligation occurs and no financial execution is inferred.
- **AC-UC-015-15 — Repeat and lost response:** given unpaid or paid cancellation committed but its response was lost, sequential/concurrent recovery with the same Buyer/identity/instructions returns the original outcome and current progress, including after the relevant cutoff; there is one closure/release and effective business audit change with no duplicate cancellation notification; one obligation belongs to a paid cancellation, while unpaid cancellation creates none solely from that action.
- **AC-UC-015-16 — Interrupted before commitment:** given an interruption before a complete cancellation is recorded, recovery rechecks current guards/time. It applies one still-eligible complete outcome or returns refusal/owning expiry/timeout; it cannot leave a paid order closed without compensation, a partial group release, or extended deadlines.
- **AC-UC-015-17 — Seller decision race:** given paid cancellation competing with Seller confirmation or permitted rejection before the deadline, one accepted outcome wins. Confirmation consumes/deducts once and prevents new ordinary cancellation; cancellation/rejection closes/releases once with one obligation and its actual accepted cause. Losing new actions add no stock, compensation or state effect.
- **AC-UC-015-18 — Seller-response boundary:** given the paid Shop response deadline has arrived with a delayed timeout worker, a new Buyer cancellation is ineligible and cannot use Buyer-cancellation reason; eligible UC-013 timeout closes/releases only with its stable required obligation. An exact replay of cancellation completed before the boundary still returns its original accepted outcome.
- **AC-UC-015-19 — Later-state cutoff:** given a Shop order already `CONFIRMED`, `PACKED`, `SHIPPED`, `DELIVERED` or later, a genuinely new ordinary cancellation is refused with no stock replenishment or payment/compensation change. An after-sales route is offered only when its separately specified UC-016/017/OQ-010 eligibility permits it.
- **AC-UC-015-20 — Delivery and completion boundary:** given correlated delivered, failed-delivery or returned evidence, tracking shows the applicable shipment progress/exception and Shop outcome without inferring Buyer completion, refund execution or inventory receipt. Refreshing/revisiting does not complete the order, restore consumed stock or invent a completion/after-sales window.
- **AC-UC-015-21 — Attributable recovery evidence:** for agreed cancellation/timeout/race/recovery records, authorized inspection correlates Buyer/owning actor, action, actual reason, effective time, operation/group/Shop identities, stock changes and compensation obligation. Effective changes remain distinguishable from rejected/repeated attempts; protected sibling facts and partner secrets are not exposed.
- **AC-UC-015-22 — Simulated boundary:** all payment evidence, compensation/reconciliation walkthroughs and shipment tracking use simulated partners; cancelling or tracking the demo performs no real-money charge/refund or real carrier action.
- **AC-UC-015-23 — Explicit Buyer completion:** given an owned Shop order in `DELIVERED` status with no active dispute case, when Buyer confirms receipt with a stable operation identity, the order transitions `DELIVERED` → `COMPLETED`; Buyer actor, effective timestamp, and operation ID are recorded; regular return window closes; physical stock and payment amounts remain unchanged.
- **AC-UC-015-24 — System auto-completion timeout:** given an owned Shop order in `DELIVERED` status with no active dispute, when the 7-day auto-completion window elapses, the system automatically transitions the order `DELIVERED` → `COMPLETED` with auto-completion timeout cause; regular return window closes.
- **AC-UC-015-25 — Active dispute suspends completion:** given an owned Shop order in `DELIVERED` status, when an eligible dispute is opened under UC-016, the auto-completion timer is suspended and explicit Buyer completion is blocked; reaching the 7-day elapsed mark does not transition the order to `COMPLETED`.
- **AC-UC-015-26 — Completion idempotency and repeat:** given a Shop order already `COMPLETED`, replaying completion under the same operation identity returns the existing completed outcome with no duplicate transition or extra business audit entry.
