## Goal

Let an authorized Seller create one whole Shipment for a paid, Seller-confirmed, packed Shop order and track its simulated physical progress. Keep partner evidence, processing status, Shipment progress, and Order progress distinguishable when requests or events are repeated, reordered, interrupted, or contradictory. Failed delivery and physical return expose an exception without claiming automatic cancellation, refund, or inventory restoration.

## Primary actor

Seller / Shop Operator with shipment-creation and fulfillment permission for the owning Shop. Exact permissions and access to delivery-address fields remain OQ-005.

## Supporting actors

- Simulated Logistics Partner acknowledges the creation request and publishes authentic, correlated tracking evidence with stable logical event identity and authoritative per-Shipment order.
- The system coordinates Shipment and Shop-order progress, records processing evidence, and recovers missing work under the original identities.
- Buyer observes permitted progress for their own purchase in [UC-015](UC-015-track-cancel-orders.md). Buyer and Seller status claims alone are not authentic Logistics Partner evidence.
- Authorized Internal Staff may inspect logistics reconciliation within OQ-005/OQ-015 permissions. A logistics exception does not grant permission to resolve a payment exception, refund, or restock; those actions remain UC-016/017 and their owning decisions.

## Trigger

The Seller requests shipment creation or records handover intent; the simulated partner acknowledges creation or delivers a tracking event; interrupted processing or a retained sequence gap is recovered.

## Preconditions

- New creation concerns one existing paid Shop order whose authorized Seller confirmation consumed its exact paid holds and deducted stock once, and whose packing completed in UC-013. New creation requires the Order to be `PACKED`; payment success alone is insufficient.
- The Order has its immutable accepted item, address, and shipping snapshot from UC-011. Shipment creation uses those facts, including exact Shop/Order identity and all its items, rather than current cart, catalog, or account-address data. Missing or incompatible required snapshot facts stop new creation for explicit resolution.
- This Proposed slice has one whole Shipment per eligible Shop order. Split shipments, item substitution, replacement shipments, and post-acceptance address/shipping edits are outside this path; they need a separately specified policy.
- Before contacting the simulator, establish a stable logical shipment-creation identity tied to the Shop order and immutable instructions. Retries or recovery use the same identity. No callback may create a Shipment merely by claiming an unknown Order or shipment reference.
- Ownership/Shop permission checks apply before responding to Seller creation, handover, or status commands. Partner evidence must pass source/authenticity and required-structure checks before it can be classified as admissible evidence. Exact signature scheme, simulator controls, callback schema, retention, and reconciliation authority remain OQ-005/OQ-012/OQ-015 and later design.
- New creation or handover commands are blocked while associated payment reconciliation is `REQUIRED`. This guard does not discard physical evidence or prevent otherwise admissible tracking progress for an already existing Shipment; any payment/logistics conflict remains explicit.

## Main flow

All new business policy in this flow is Proposed and references the canonical rules below.

1. The Seller requests creation for their `PACKED` Shop order. After authorization, resolve the original operation identity and immutable instructions before new-creation state guards. A completed repeat returns the same Shipment and current Order progress without another simulator creation. Conflicting reuse is refused; a new or uncommitted request must satisfy the creation guards.
2. The system establishes or recovers one logical Shipment in `CREATION_PENDING`, tied to that Order and its full accepted snapshot, before contacting the simulated Logistics Partner. It requests creation under the stable identity. The Order remains `PACKED`; quantities, paid amount, and already consumed reservations do not change.
3. The partner returns an authentic creation acknowledgement correlated to the existing logical creation identity, Shipment, and Order. Apply it once to establish `AWAITING_PICKUP`, the partner reference, and the authoritative sequence baseline. Exact sequence format and starting number belong to the agreed protocol; unavailable sequencing is an exception to recover, not a guessed order. Lost or uncertain creation responses follow A2.
4. The Seller may record permitted handover intent against that Shipment. Intent says that pickup is requested or the parcel is prepared for handover; it is not evidence of physical pickup and changes neither the Shipment to `PICKED_UP` nor the Order to `SHIPPED`.
5. The partner publishes a tracking event. Verify source/authenticity and required structure first. For an already-known provider-scoped event identity, compare immutable evidence with the original record before new-event correlation/state guards: an exact repeat returns its recorded processing outcome and current progress; changed evidence requires reconciliation tied to the original association without applying the changed payload or exposing another purchase.
6. For a new authenticated event identity, correlate the existing Shipment, exact owning Shop order, and partner reference, and validate its authoritative per-Shipment sequence. Unknown/mismatched association is rejected without Shipment/Order effects. A gap or missing predecessor is retained as deferred evidence without public progress; A7 recovers the missing permitted path. Event timestamps do not decide transition order.
7. Apply admissible contiguous tracking evidence through [SM-SHIPMENT-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-shipment-001). Authentic `PICKED_UP` evidence moves `AWAITING_PICKUP` → `PICKED_UP` and the matching Order `PACKED` → `SHIPPED` together once. `IN_TRANSIT` advances Shipment progress while the Order stays `SHIPPED`. Do not apply another stock deduction, reservation consumption, or payment effect.
8. Authentic admissible `DELIVERED` evidence moves the Shipment and matching Order to `DELIVERED` together once. Delivery is distinct from commercial completion; it does not set `COMPLETED`, close an after-sales window, recognize revenue, or imply a refund disposition. Those choices remain OQ-010 and UC-015/016/017/023.
9. The system exposes authorized current Shipment/Order progress and any deferred/reconciliation status separately, and records the correlated accepted effect and notification intent once. Partner delivery attempts and retained evidence remain distinguishable from an effective business change.

## Alternative and error flows

- **A1 — Unauthorized action or protected lookup:** refuse cross-Shop creation, handover, status access, or staff intervention without revealing another Buyer's address, sibling-Shop records, or unrestricted payment-group data. Simulator-control authority is separate from Shop permissions and remains OQ-005.
- **A2 — Unknown creation result or lost response:** keep `CREATION_PENDING` and recover/query/retry the same logical creation identity. A local transport failure does not prove that the partner created nothing. A correlated acknowledgement recovers that Shipment to `AWAITING_PICKUP` once; never issue a second logical Shipment to escape uncertainty. Proven creation rejection remains an explicit pending-creation exception with permitted retry/disposition unresolved under OQ-015; it does not cancel the Order or authorize a replacement identity.
- **A3 — Ineligible new creation:** refuse creation for unpaid, awaiting-confirmation, rejected, cancelled, timed-out, unpacked, or otherwise closed Orders, for a second Shipment, for partial-item instructions, or for missing/incompatible snapshot facts. Do not invent new stock, payment, address, or shipping facts. Return a permitted existing result before these new-action guards when recovering an already completed operation.
- **A4 — Payment reconciliation blocks a new command:** refuse new creation/handover while payment reconciliation is `REQUIRED`, with a permission-scoped explanation and no new command effect. If a Shipment already exists, still retain later authentic physical evidence and apply its allowed contiguous progress. The payment exception cannot erase pickup/delivery history or falsely keep an already picked-up parcel merely awaiting pickup; payment resolution remains independent.
- **A5 — Malformed, unauthenticated, or uncorrelated evidence:** reject source/structure failures without Shipment/Order effects or a reconciliation conflict solely from that claim. For a new authenticated identity, reject unknown/mismatched Shipment/Order association before effects. A callback cannot create a Shipment, change ownership, or adopt a reference that was never tied to the original creation. Authenticated changed evidence under a known event identity follows A8; that original-record comparison precedes new-event correlation.
- **A6 — Exact repeat or consistent extra evidence:** an exact authenticated replay returns its recorded processing outcome and current progress before new-transition guards, including after later progress. A new event ID that reports a consistent already-applied state may retain evidence without another business effect; sequence/association checks still apply. Neither case regresses Order progress or repeats audit/notification/stock effects. Equivalent current-state evidence at the next contiguous sequence accounts for that sequence as a no-op; old/exact replays do not advance it twice. A true failed-delivery retry follows the graph rather than being suppressed because IN_TRANSIT appeared earlier. A repeat of deferred evidence remains deferred until its gap is legitimately recovered.
- **A7 — Gap or unavailable sequence baseline:** retain authentic correlated evidence without treating the claimed future state as current progress. Recover the missing predecessor events through replay or a permission-scoped authoritative partner query that supplies the full permitted path and ordering evidence. Apply contiguous allowed transitions once, then re-evaluate deferred evidence against current sequence/state. A latest-status-only answer, unavailable predecessor, or ambiguous baseline does not justify timestamp sorting or a jump over required transitions; expose unresolved recovery/reconciliation instead.
- **A8 — Conflicting identity, sequence, or immutable facts:** authenticated reuse of a known event ID with changed evidence requires reconciliation on its original association. Different events claiming incompatible facts at the same sequence, a contradictory old event, inconsistent creation acknowledgement, or an inadmissible contiguous transition also require explicit reconciliation. Preserve original effects and evidence; do not apply changed payloads, remap the Shipment/Order, reset sequence, or blindly overwrite progress.
- **A9 — Reordered older event:** if authenticated correlated evidence is behind the committed sequence, compare it with accepted history. Retain a consistent older event as historical evidence without regressing current states or repeating effects. Missing or contradictory history follows A7/A8; older occurrence timestamps alone do not prove compatibility.
- **A10 — Failed delivery and retry:** admissible `DELIVERY_FAILED` evidence moves an in-transit Shipment to nonterminal `DELIVERY_FAILED`, with an observable shipment exception; Order remains `SHIPPED`. Later contiguous permitted retry evidence may move the Shipment to `IN_TRANSIT` and later `DELIVERED`. Failed delivery alone is neither Order cancellation nor a return/refund decision, and it changes no stock or payment amount. Attempt limits and who initiates retry remain OQ-010/OQ-015.
- **A11 — Physical return:** admissible evidence moves `DELIVERY_FAILED` → `RETURNING` → `RETURNED`. Order stays `SHIPPED` with the shipment exception through `RETURNING`; authentic admissible `RETURNED` evidence moves the Order to `DELIVERY_EXCEPTION` together with Shipment `RETURNED`. A physical return is not automatic cancellation, refund completion, commercial case closure, or inventory receipt/restock. UC-016/017/OQ-010 must specify those separate decisions and verified goods receipt.
- **A12 — Late evidence after final logistics outcome:** ordinary callbacks cannot overwrite final `DELIVERED` or `RETURNED` with an opposite or stale state. Consistent repeat/same-outcome evidence returns or retains the established outcome without effects. Authentic contradictory final evidence requires reconciliation; a delivered Shipment cannot enter an after-sales return through this ordinary delivery graph. No blanket ignore rule discards genuine conflicting partner evidence.
- **A13 — Concurrent delivery, creation, or terminal evidence:** resolve competing requests/events through the same original identity, sequence, and transition guards. At most one creation and one effect per accepted progress transition occur; incompatible terminal evidence follows A8/A12. Never expose Shipment pickup/delivery as successfully completed while the matching Order remains in the preceding state due to partial processing.
- **A14 — Processing interruption:** recover the same creation/event identity, established business effects, current committed sequence, and current Order/Shipment state. If only receipt/verification was recorded, that event is not yet public progress; apply it only if it remains contiguous and allowed, defer it for its gap, or reconcile a conflict. If its effect already committed, return current progress and complete only genuinely missing defined processing work once. Restart does not repeat inventory/payment changes, notification intent, or effective audit records.
- **A15 — Later source-data change:** continue with the accepted Order/shipping snapshot. A current Product/address edit cannot rewrite an existing Shipment or silently reroute a parcel. Shop/staff restrictions and authorized corrections to an existing delivery require explicit OQ-004/OQ-005/OQ-007/OQ-009/OQ-010/OQ-015 disposition; this path does not infer one.

## State/data changes

- [SM-SHIPMENT-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-shipment-001) owns `CREATION_PENDING`, `AWAITING_PICKUP`, `PICKED_UP`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `DELIVERY_FAILED`, `RETURNING`, `DELIVERED`, and `RETURNED`. Processing classifications such as deferred/rejected/reconciliation are distinguishable from physical states. Seller handover intent is not a physical-state transition.
- [SM-ORDER-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-order-001) owns `PACKED` → `SHIPPED` on authentic admissible pickup, `SHIPPED` → `DELIVERED` on admissible delivery, and `SHIPPED` → `DELIVERY_EXCEPTION` on admissible physical return. Pickup/delivery/return effects change Shipment and matching Shop order together once. Failure/returning retains `SHIPPED` with an observable shipment exception; sibling Shop orders are unchanged.
- [SM-PAYMENT-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-payment-001) and [SM-RESERVATION-001](../STATE_MACHINES.md#sm-reservation-001) remain unchanged by ordinary logistics effects. Payment reconciliation is a separate guard/annotation; Shipment progress cannot close it. No Shipment event restores consumed reservations or creates stock movement.
- Preserve the original logical creation identity, Shop/Order/Shipment association, accepted snapshot reference, partner identity/reference, provider-scoped immutable event ID/evidence, authoritative sequence/baseline, processing classification, prior/current states, and correlated effects for recovery. Exact formats, storage, transaction/queue mechanisms, signature/authentication protocol, and payload schemas belong to later design.
- Proposed audit coverage distinguishes creation/recovery, handover intent, accepted progress, deferred/rejected evidence, history-only evidence, and reconciliation requirement. Effective changes record actor or simulated partner, action, changed data/outcome, timestamp, and the affected Shipment/Order once; callback deliveries and evidence retention are not repeated effective changes. Do not record verification secrets. Address visibility, redaction, retention, diagnostics access, and rejected-attempt coverage remain OQ-005/OQ-012/OQ-015.

## Postconditions

- Creation acknowledged: one Shipment `AWAITING_PICKUP` for the complete `PACKED` Shop order, with original immutable facts and recoverable identity; no second Shipment, stock change, or payment effect.
- Pickup/transit: authentic permitted pickup synchronizes Shipment pickup and Order `SHIPPED`; transit is Shipment progress under that shipped Order. Handover intent alone leaves physical progress unchanged.
- Delivered: Shipment and Order `DELIVERED`; commercial completion/after-sales policy is pending separately. Failed/returning progress keeps Order `SHIPPED` with the exception; physical return produces Shipment `RETURNED` and Order `DELIVERY_EXCEPTION`.
- Deferred/reconciliation: correlated evidence and established progress are preserved, with an observable permitted exception. No false current pickup/delivery, unsafe transition, automatic refund/cancellation/restock, or exception closure is claimed.
- Rejected/unauthorized action: protected Shipment, Order, payment, and inventory data remain unchanged; diagnostic visibility follows the eventual permission policy.

## Acceptance criteria

All criteria below are **Proposed** specification examples, not executed test evidence. Unless stated otherwise, each scenario uses authentic simulator evidence with exact existing Shipment/Order association and an authoritative sequence baseline. Signature protocol, sequence encoding, retry limits, and permission fixtures remain open. Rules/states/NFRs are linked in the coverage mapping below.

- **AC-UC-014-01 — Whole paid packed Order:** given a paid Seller-confirmed `PACKED` Shop order, when its authorized operator creates a Shipment, exactly one logical whole Shipment contains all its accepted items and the same Shop/Order association; the Order remains `PACKED`, with no new deduction, release, or payment effect.
- **AC-UC-014-02 — Shipment permission:** given an unrelated Shop order or Buyer's Shipment, when an unauthorized operator/Buyer/staff actor requests creation, handover, or status, protected address, sibling-Shop, and payment-group data are not disclosed and no protected business data changes.
- **AC-UC-014-03 — Creation eligibility:** given an unpaid, awaiting-confirmation, rejected, cancelled, Seller-timed-out, or unpacked Order, when a new creation is requested, no Shipment or simulator creation occurs. A partial-item request or second logical Shipment for an existing Order is also refused.
- **AC-UC-014-04 — Stable creation identity and replay:** given an eligible Order, when creation is requested repeatedly or concurrently under its original immutable instructions, the logical identity exists before partner contact and every response/recovery refers to one Shipment. A completed repeat after later progress returns current progress without new-state guards or another creation; conflicting reuse is refused without rewriting facts.
- **AC-UC-014-05 — Unknown creation outcome:** given the simulator created a Shipment but the response was lost, when recovery queries/retries, it uses the original creation identity and recovers the correlated acknowledgement to `AWAITING_PICKUP` once. Uncertainty remains observable until established; transport failure never creates a replacement identity or cancels the Order.
- **AC-UC-014-06 — Immutable shipping snapshot:** given an accepted Order and later changes to Buyer address, Product descriptions, or shipping choices, when creation/recovery occurs, it uses the original accepted Order/address/shipping facts. Missing/incompatible snapshot facts stop a new command for explicit resolution rather than silently taking current data.
- **AC-UC-014-07 — Creation acknowledgement:** given `CREATION_PENDING`, when an authentic acknowledgement matches its original creation/Shipment/Order association, it establishes `AWAITING_PICKUP`, partner reference, and authoritative sequence baseline once. Unknown/mismatched acknowledgement cannot create or remap a Shipment; unavailable sequencing is recovered explicitly before tracking effects depend on it.
- **AC-UC-014-08 — Handover intent:** given `AWAITING_PICKUP`, when an authorized Seller records handover intent repeatedly, the permitted intent is attributable once and physical Shipment progress stays `AWAITING_PICKUP`; Order remains `PACKED` until authentic admissible pickup evidence.
- **AC-UC-014-09 — Authentic pickup and transit:** given `AWAITING_PICKUP` with a `PACKED` Order, when contiguous permitted `PICKED_UP` evidence arrives, Shipment `PICKED_UP` and Order `SHIPPED` commit together once. Later contiguous `IN_TRANSIT` changes Shipment progress while Order stays `SHIPPED`, without stock/payment effects.
- **AC-UC-014-10 — Delivery differs from completion:** given an in-transit Shipment and `SHIPPED` Order, when contiguous permitted delivery evidence arrives, Shipment and Order become `DELIVERED` together once. They do not become `COMPLETED`, refunded, or restocked; sibling Orders and unresolved financial cases remain distinct.
- **AC-UC-014-11 — Evidence rejection:** malformed/missing-identity or unauthenticated evidence changes no Shipment/Order or reconciliation case solely from that claim. A new authenticated identity with unknown/mismatched Shipment/Order association is also rejected without effects; it cannot create a Shipment or disclose another purchase.
- **AC-UC-014-12 — Exact event replay before current-state guards:** given an accepted pickup event and subsequent delivery, when its identical authenticated provider-scoped event is replayed sequentially or concurrently, it returns its original processing outcome and current delivered progress without another transition, deduction, effective audit change, or notification intent. A deferred event repeat stays deferred until its gap is recovered.
- **AC-UC-014-13 — Changed known identity:** given an authenticated recorded event, when the same provider-scoped ID carries changed status, sequence, Shipment/Order association, or immutable facts, reconciliation is tied to its original record before new-event correlation guards. The changed payload is not applied and cannot reveal the claimed other purchase; unauthenticated copying of that ID cannot create the conflict.
- **AC-UC-014-14 — Sequence gap:** given pickup has not yet been applied, when an authentic later transit/delivery event arrives with a missing predecessor, it is retained as deferred and current Shipment/Order progress does not advance or claim pickup/delivery. An earlier claimed occurrence timestamp cannot make it eligible.
- **AC-UC-014-15 — Recover the full permitted path:** given retained future evidence, when missing events replay or an authorized authoritative query supplies the missing allowed path and ordering facts, each contiguous permitted transition applies once and the deferred event is re-evaluated. A latest-status-only response or unknown baseline cannot skip pickup/transit or force a terminal jump; unresolved recovery remains observable.
- **AC-UC-014-16 — Consistent older evidence:** given later committed progress, when a new authenticated event reports an older sequence consistent with recorded history, it is retained as historical evidence without regression or repeated effects. A contradictory old event or incompatible events at the same sequence require reconciliation; timestamp sorting cannot resolve them.
- **AC-UC-014-17 — Consistent extra same-outcome evidence:** given an applied correlated progress/outcome, when a different authenticated event ID reports consistent same-state evidence under admissible sequence/history checks, current progress is returned and evidence is retained without another business effect. Equivalent current-state evidence at the next contiguous sequence is recorded as processed without a repeated physical/Order effect, allowing a subsequent legitimate sequence without a false gap. Older/exact replays cannot advance it twice; a true failed-delivery retry still applies its graph transition. A different ID does not bypass association/sequence checks or make contradictory evidence valid.
- **AC-UC-014-18 — Nonterminal failed delivery and retry:** given `IN_TRANSIT`, when contiguous permitted failure evidence arrives, Shipment becomes `DELIVERY_FAILED` and Order remains `SHIPPED` with an observable exception. Later permitted retry evidence can return Shipment to `IN_TRANSIT` and then delivery, without cancellation, refund, paid-hold release, or restock caused by those logistics events.
- **AC-UC-014-19 — Return exception:** given `DELIVERY_FAILED`, when permitted contiguous returning and returned evidence arrives, Shipment moves through `RETURNING` to `RETURNED`; Order stays `SHIPPED` during returning and becomes `DELIVERY_EXCEPTION` with returned progress. No stock, payment, refund-completion, or commercial-closure effect follows automatically.
- **AC-UC-014-20 — Final progress protection:** given Shipment `DELIVERED` or `RETURNED`, when ordinary authentic opposite-final or inconsistent stale evidence arrives, established progress is preserved and reconciliation is observable. Exact/consistent same-outcome evidence has no new effect; ordinary delivery callbacks cannot start an after-sales return from delivered progress.
- **AC-UC-014-21 — Interrupted and concurrent processing:** given concurrent event deliveries or an interruption after verified receipt but before effects, recovery uses the original event/current sequence/state and applies a still-contiguous permitted effect once, defers its gap, or reconciles a conflict. Recorded receipt alone never shows public physical progress. A committed pickup/delivery/return outcome preserves its paired Order update and completes only missing defined work without repeated effects.
- **AC-UC-014-22 — Payment exception versus physical evidence:** given payment reconciliation `REQUIRED`, a new creation/handover command is refused without effects. Given an already existing Shipment, later authentic correlated contiguous pickup/transit/delivery/return evidence remains retained and applies allowed progress while the payment exception stays explicit; logistics cannot resolve or erase financial evidence.
- **AC-UC-014-23 — Audit and observable processing:** for creation/recovery, handover intent, accepted progress, deferred/history-only/rejected evidence, and reconciliation, permitted inspection can distinguish the actor/partner, action, time, association, processing classification, and changed outcome. Each effective Shipment/Order/audit/notification effect occurs once, with delivery attempts separate and verification secrets excluded; protected address access remains permission-scoped.
- **AC-UC-014-24 — Per-Shop and compensation isolation:** given two paid Shop orders under one group payment, when one Shipment delivers, fails, or returns, only its matched Order/Shipment progress changes. The sibling's progress, group payment application, and inventory remain unchanged; physical outcomes do not report a refund completed, restore consumed holds, or claim commercially completed Orders.
